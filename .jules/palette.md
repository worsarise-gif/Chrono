## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA label in Mode Selector\n**Learning:** The Mode selector dropdown relies on visual `<Check>` icons and button backgrounds, but lacks clear ARIA attributes or screen-reader context for the current selection state, especially on mobile where it switches layouts.\n**Action:** Always ensure ARIA labels indicate the purpose of buttons, especially when relying on dynamic icons, and provide `aria-current` or `aria-selected` for selected states in dropdown menus.
